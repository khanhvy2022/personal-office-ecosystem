## 2. Investigation of Manifest Merger Warnings

During Gradle build, the following warnings were observed:
```text
uses-permission#android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE was tagged at AndroidManifest.xml to remove other declarations but no other declaration present
uses-permission#android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE was tagged at AndroidManifest.xml to remove other declarations but no other declaration present
uses-permission#android.permission.INTERNET was tagged at AndroidManifest.xml to remove other declarations but no other declaration present
uses-permission#android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE was tagged at AndroidManifest.xml to remove other declarations but no other declaration present
uses-permission#android.permission.MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE was tagged at AndroidManifest.xml to remove other declarations but no other declaration present
```

### Cause & Technical Significance:
1. `tools:node="remove"` tells Android's manifest merger to strip the specified permission if it is introduced by any imported library (such as `:engine:collabora-runtime` or transitive AndroidX dependencies).
2. When the library itself does NOT declare the permission, Android's manifest merger emits an informational warning notifying the developer that no declaration was found to remove.
3. This is an intended defensive guardrail: it guarantees that even if a future dependency update introduces `INTERNET` or `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, the manifest merger will actively strip it.
4. **Security Confirmation:** The permissions are verified 100% absent in the final APK binary manifests (`aapt2 dump permissions`).
